# Programming conventions and swap standards

## Workout entry

- Several movements in one combined cell are listed with **•** bullets.
- Warm-up and cool-down are usually **No Score** (no format).
- Exercises like banded leg curls are bilateral unless the notes say otherwise; known unilateral ones (e.g. lunges)
  count **per side**.
- **Barbell complexes** are combined blocks (like metcons) scored by load. Set the % of 1RM from the sets/reps, with a
  note telling the client to apply it to the 1RM of their **weakest** lift in the complex.
- Remove notes that reference upcoming workouts.

## Swap standards

- **Muscle-up** → 1 pull-up + 1 dip each (1:1).
- **Double-unders** → single-unders, or double-under practice for the average time the double-under reps would take.
- **Cable tricep pushdowns** → banded pushdowns when there's no cable machine (banded pushdowns can also be programmed
  on their own).
