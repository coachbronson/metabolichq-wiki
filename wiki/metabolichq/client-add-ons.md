# Client add-ons (#382, medium priority)

"Add-on marketplace" = paid add-ons MetabolicHQ sells directly to clients.

## Rules

- Offered in every partner space; coaches can't hide or turn them off.
- Partners get no revenue share unless separately agreed; partner agreement says so (add-on clause).
- Buying one creates no coaching relationship with MetabolicHQ. MetabolicHQ handles support and refunds.
- Coaches see their clients' add-on data as **derived results only** (e.g. oxalate totals/trends/signals, never
  licensed source values); it can feed signals and achievements. Each add-on's config says which signals/report data
  coaches get, and the coach is notified when a client buys one that opens new insights.
- Configured on an **add-ons page** under the Platform part of the portal; it creates the Stripe products/prices (like
  coaching packages). Partner coaches don't get an add-ons page. Coach websites (#523) are not client add-ons.
- Open question: how clients learn add-ons exist.

## Coach add-ons (planned)

Coach add-ons are the coach-side counterpart: bought by a space's main coach, billed to the coach, used with the
coach's clients. Not client add-ons (#633).

- **Coach add-on marketplace** (#633, low): a Platform → Coach add-ons catalog for Bronson; coaches add and cancel
  modules in an **Add-ons section on Space settings** (decided 2026-11-15). Flat monthly prices, any tier; MPT
  Programming (Premium) moves onto it unchanged.
- **GLP-1 companion** (#628, low; sub-issues #629–#632): coaches assign it to clients on GLP-1 medications; logs
  medication and protocol, reads it in nutrition, training, biometrics and signals; muscle preservation and an
  off-ramp. Price decided: **$57.99/month** flat (2026-11-15, #628). It logs only: never prescribes or suggests doses;
  MPT Coach gives a fixed "ask your prescriber" reply; client consent on assignment; no achievements for weight lost
  (#628).
- Ideas needing a decision: Challenges (#639), Fasting tracker (#640), client progress troubleshooting with AI
  feedback (#642, client or coach add-on).
