# Client add-ons (#382, now high priority as a feature)

"Add-on marketplace" = paid add-ons MetabolicHQ sells directly to clients.

## Rules

- Offered in every partner space; coaches can't hide or turn them off.
- Partners get no revenue share unless separately agreed; partner agreement says so (add-on clause).
- Buying one creates no coaching relationship with MetabolicHQ. MetabolicHQ handles support and refunds.
- Coaches see their clients' add-on data as **derived results only** (e.g. oxalate totals/trends/signals, never
  licensed source values); it can feed signals and achievements. Each add-on's config says which signals/report data
  coaches get, and the coach is notified when a client buys one that opens new insights.
- Configured on an **add-ons page** under the Platform part of the portal; it creates the Stripe products/prices (like
  coaching packages). Partner coaches don't get an add-ons page. Coach websites (#523) are not client add-ons.
- Open question: how clients learn add-ons exist.
