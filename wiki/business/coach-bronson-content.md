# Coach Bronson content (athlete brand)

Master plan: **Coach Bronson Content Strategy & 13-Week Calendar**
(https://claude.ai/artifact/K9wnsFJ6aGvaxmeG14F8uK). Strategy source doc: Google Doc "Content Guideline".

## Positioning and goals

- **Audience:** high-intensity athletes and their coaches (CrossFit, HYROX, MMA/combat, tactical); the site and blog
  focus on Masters athletes 35+.
- **Positioning:** the research voice arguing that the way high-intensity athletes are fed is shortening their careers. A
  scientist who publishes the studies, with twenty years of applied results.
- **Tagline** on every cover and final slide: *"The cost of performance shouldn't be quality of life."* (replaces the old
  "Fitness is Freedom", which is retired everywhere).
- **Goals, in order:** an engaged audience of athletes and coaches; expert authority in metabolic performance and
  longevity; qualified athlete inquiries; an audience for *The New Essentials of Sports Nutrition* (late 2028).
  (Masters Collective sign-ups were a goal until its shutdown on 2026-11-14.)
- **Filter:** every post traces to at least one key message. No weight-loss framing, no sales-page tone, no
  education-only weeks.

## The 8 key messages

01 Food is not fuel, it is recovery · 02 Recovery is the foundation, training is the stimulus · 03 Biology uses
nutrition as a recovery tool · 04 Carb-centric protocols lower the ceiling and raise risk · 05 Whole-food,
animal-based, low-carb is the foundation · 06 Only work as hard as you need to · 07 Sleep · 08 Everything is stress.

## Weekly template (5 posts, Mon–Fri)

| Day | Format | Pillar | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Talking-head (TH) reel | Cost of performance | Strongest moment from the latest Riverside recording |
| Tue | Carousel (3–5 slides) | Research | One paper: what it tested, what it found, what it means for the athlete |
| Wed | B-roll voice-over (BV) reel | Reframe | One misused term or claim, voice from a recording over gym B-roll |
| Thu | TH or carousel | Proof and story | An athlete, a client result or his own story |
| Fri | (was Masters Collective) | — | Needs a new plan |

- Mix: roughly 60% reach and authority, 20% human, 20% offer.
- **Never two carousels in a row.** Tuesday is the only carousel early in the week; if Thursday is a carousel, Friday is a
  TH. Monday is always a TH.

## Production rules

- Every video gets a cover image with a hook, built from Drive's Images folder; the hook sits inside the 4:5 crop. No
  opening on-screen hook text on videos.
- TH captions: Poppins ExtraBold 800, size 50, white uppercase, 3D shadow, word highlight purple `#7C33ED`, one line per
  segment, width 0.62, rounded corners, y 0.7 (from his Riverside reference edit).
- Logo without the old tagline; brand blue, charcoal and red.
- Blur member names and faces in any community screenshots.
- B-roll is reused and recut rather than shooting more.
- Weekly delivery: Drive › Social Media Share › Automated Content › [week folder]: Video, Images, Captions (one file with
  every post's text, hashtags, date and filenames). TH videos come from Riverside; he uploads them.
- Wants samples before anything is published.

## Platforms and captions

- Instagram, Facebook (page "Evidence Based Sports Performance") and LinkedIn. The same video/images go to all three;
  one Instagram/Facebook caption plus a shorter, more professional LinkedIn version.
- Publer is **no longer used** (dropped Nov 11, 2026); the strategy doc still says Publer.
- Hashtags: Instagram 5 (post-specific; they don't add reach), Facebook 1–2, LinkedIn 3. Search phrases ("keto for
  CrossFit athletes", "carbs and performance") go in the caption text. Alt text on every post. Instagram captions point
  to the link in bio.

## Book breadcrumbs

*The New Essentials of Sports Nutrition* shows up about once a week as a **source, never a pitch**: a closing line on a
research or reframe caption ("I go deeper on this in a chapter of my book…"), proof posts opening "One of the athletes
profiled in my book…", a carousel slide credit ("From The New Essentials of Sports Nutrition, in progress."). No dates,
prices or preorder language until there's something to sign up for.

Chapter → pillar map (May 2026 draft): 1 Understanding Metabolism → 2, 3 (fuel vs. function, oxidative priority) ·
2 Athlete-Centered Nutrition → 1 · 3 Basics of Nutrition → 3 · 5 Keto Myths → 3 · 6 Fat Adaptation → 1, 3 ·
7 What Science Gets Wrong → 2 · 8 Performance and Sustainability → 1, 2 · 13 You Might Need More Fat → 3 ·
14 Profiles of Keto-Adapted Athletes → 4 (15 athletes, Zach Bitter to Jocelyn Rylee). Athlete facts get checked against a
current source before posting.

## coachbronson.com blog (drafting paused)

Rules in `coachbronson-site/content/DRAFTING.md`:
- Every other Monday; Claude drafts from `content/blog-topics.md`, Bronson approves by merging. **Drafting is paused**
  (`blog_automation: false`); approved posts still go live.
- Audience: Masters athletes 35+ in CrossFit, HYROX, combat sports and tactical; keto up front.
- One SEO keyword per post chosen from real searches; 650–900 words; first person as Bronson.
- Ends with one next step: MPT free trial first, then the free guide; 1:1 coaching only for posts about individual
  coaching. Never the Masters Collective.
- Every study or statistic web-checked and linked; past clients anonymous; no invented testimonials.
- Covers picked from his Drive photo folder; no reuse.
- Topics queued: starting keto mid-season; protein for Masters athletes; recovery after 40 as a nutrition problem (tie in
  MPT); keto for combat-sports two-a-days.

## Weekly newsletter (Thursdays)

Rules in `coachbronson-site/content/NEWSLETTER.md`: one main idea (300–500 words), the latest blog post, one call to
action (`mpt`, `guide` or `call`; not the same three weeks running), and one partner deal from the app's Deals in
rotation. Drafted about a week ahead from `content/newsletter-topics.md`; approved in the site portal. Weekly site report
Fridays.
