# Content and social media

## Approvals and schedules

- Separate schedules: blogs for MetabolicHQ and Coach Bronson, social for both, newsletter for Coach Bronson only.
- He approves every item as it's created. A weekly email lists anything not yet approved.
- Wants a regular posting schedule for both brands, each with its own dashboard to manage and approve content.
- Publer dropped for both brands.

## Coach Bronson social

- Accounts: Facebook page "Evidence Based Sports Performance", Instagram and LinkedIn (credentials DHSc(c), CSCS,
  TSAC-F, CCFT), YouTube @coach.bronson. Posts go to Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.
- Audience (Content Guideline doc): high-intensity athletes and their coaches.
- Content types: talking-head reel with cover image, 3–5 image carousel, B-roll with voice-over. Every post has a
  cover image with a hook; no on-screen hook text in the video. Never post carousels back to back. B-roll can be
  reused and recut.
- Talking-head caption style (from his Riverside reference edit): Poppins ExtraBold 800, size 50, white uppercase, 3D
  shadow, word highlight in purple `#7C33ED`, one line per segment, width 0.62, rounded corners, y 0.7.
- Hashtags: few and post-specific (Instagram ≤5, Facebook 1–2, LinkedIn 3); search phrases in captions; alt text on
  every post.
- At least one post a week promotes the Masters Collective interest list. Leave breadcrumbs about his book.
- Blur member names/faces in Circle screenshots.
- Storage: Google Drive "Social Media Share › Automated Content", one folder per week with subfolders per content
  type; talking-head videos come from Riverside (he uploads them).
- Future: talk about Voice Studio.

## MetabolicHQ social and blog

- Own accounts (not his athlete accounts). Audience: coaches and coaching businesses. Not "prelaunch" — coaches can
  sign up free now.
- Photorealistic character images of coaches voicing a problem MetabolicHQ fixes, plus the client angle (no more
  juggling apps). Instagram folder in Drive; file names by topic, numbered.
- Core story: founded by a 20-year coach frustrated by app companies ignoring coaches; built on relationships.
- Underlying message: many coaches run on spreadsheets and video calls, can't connect data, and have no intelligence
  helping them; show (with some FOMO) what technology and AI can do, and how low the barrier is.
- Ground content in his book's concepts ([Coaching methods](coaching-methods.md)).

## MetabolicHQ messaging framework (Nov 13, 2026)

**One claim:** "Run your whole coaching business from one place."

**Five pillars** (rotate across all content): **Get paid** (packages, Stripe checkout, website payment widgets, no
platform fee) · **Deliver the coaching** (programming, in-app calls with AI notes, scheduling, DMs, resources,
community) · **See the whole client** (training, macros + whole-food %, glucose/ketones/GKI/HRV, check-ins, signals) ·
**Grow as a team** (multi-coach, assignment, limits per business) · **Own it** (export, no lock-in, fundamentals
included, free up to 5 clients).

**Channels:** website "Replace your stack" section (tool logos collapsing into one MetHQ screen, pillar tiles with real
screenshots, stack-cost comparison) — **first focus**; blog "Day in the life of a MetHQ coach" series plus pillar and
mission posts; social one pillar per post with 15–30 s screen recordings; Coach Bronson newsletter gets an "Are you a
coach?" footer and behind-the-build stories. Prefer proof from early partners over feature claims.
