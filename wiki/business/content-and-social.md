# Content and social media

Two brands, separate audiences, separate accounts:

- **Coach Bronson** (athletes): [Coach Bronson content](coach-bronson-content.md): positioning, tagline, key messages,
  pillars, weekly social template, production rules, book breadcrumbs, blog and newsletter rules.
- **MetabolicHQ** (coaches): [MetabolicHQ content](../metabolichq/methq-content.md): messaging framework, homepage,
  social, blog rules and topic queue.

## How it runs

- Separate schedules: blogs for both brands (alternating Mondays), social for both, a Thursday newsletter for Coach
  Bronson only.
- Claude drafts; Bronson approves every item before it goes out. A weekly email lists anything not yet approved
  (workouts, blogs, social posts, newsletters).
- Daily scheduled tasks: "Blog and newsletter drafts" (coachbronson.com) and "MetHQ Blog Drafts" (metabolichq.app).
- Publer is not used for either brand.
- Riverside is the recording studio; Drive is the central store; Voice Studio is something he wants to talk about later.
- Videos (demo video, voice-overs, footage, YouTube): [Video production](video-production.md).
