# Coaching methods

From his book draft *The New Essentials of Sports Nutrition* (v5, Oct 2026). Read before writing MetabolicHQ or
coaching content about metabolic health.

- **Metabolism is fuel and function, not a furnace.** Nutrition enables the body's functions and supplies the energy
  (ATP) to perform them. Food is functional material, not just energy.
- **Systems of function:** biology (cellular repair, hormones, inflammation, mitochondria), physiology
  (cardiovascular, fluid balance, thermoregulation, endocrine output), neurology (central drive, autonomic balance,
  sleep, perception of effort).
- **Functional macros** (protein) vs **fuel macros** (fat and carbs); they can be managed independently.
- **Macros over calories:** the body processes nutrients, not calories; calorie counting is a "SWAG"; energy intake and
  nutrition are not the same thing.
- **Oxidative priority** is processing order, not preference; external substrates always interrupt internal
  processing.
- **True metabolic flexibility:** coordinated storage, access, production and use of substrates; less dependence on
  constant external supply; eat to store for later use rather than for each session.
- **Athlete-centered nutrition:** every decision judged twice — does it support today's training, and does it preserve
  or improve basic metabolic function and long-term health?
- **Physiological resilience:** stability of biology, physiology and neurology under load over time; judge the whole
  athlete before, during and after training, not output alone.
- **Adaptive economy:** how efficiently those systems coordinate under stress. Strong = meets demands with less
  disruption and recovers efficiently; poor = similar output at greater cost (glycemic volatility, autonomic strain,
  inflammation, impaired recovery, substrate dependency).
- **The longevity band:** adaptive economy sets the adaptive bandwidth; goal is to train consistently, recover
  efficiently and stay metabolically stable for years, leaving sport healthier than you entered.
- **Ketogenic adaptation phases:** fat-adapted → keto-adapted → keto-optimized. Ketosis is not adaptation. Lean
  athletes often need more fat.
- **Simple macro formula:** lean mass in lb → grams; protein at least that, carbs + fat no more than that; at least
  100 g fat; about 50 g carbs or less to stay fat-adapted. Lean, hard-training athletes often need more fat.
- **Carbohydrate is a targeted tool:** minimum effective dose, mainly for recovery; 10–20 g/h to start in long
  endurance efforts; post-event carbs tapering off before the next event; carbs after training to hold body mass.
- **Watch the trend, not one number:** HRV-CV and repeated heart-rate-recovery tests show rising physiological cost.
- Metabolic health is the foundation of all performance, in competition or in life.

Full chapter summary and the draft's open edits: [The New Essentials of Sports Nutrition](new-essentials-book.md).

How MetabolicHQ reflects these: macros in grams, calories off by default, whole-food %, signals against each client's
own normal, no achievements for under-eating or weight loss.
